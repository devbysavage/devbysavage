## <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/01-10B981?style=flat-square" /> About Me

I'm **Jobi F. Ayomide**, also known as **Savage**.

My journey into technology didn't begin with code. It began with creativity, visual storytelling, content creation, and finding ways to help ideas connect with people.

That creative foundation led me to web development. Today, I'm developing my technical skills and turning ideas into responsive interfaces, useful applications, and backend systems.

I enjoy working at the intersection of **technology, creativity, and communication** — where good engineering meets thoughtful design.

- Building frontend experiences and full-stack applications.
- Developing REST APIs and working with databases.
- Learning through practical projects, testing, and debugging.
- Exploring blockchain development as a longer-term interest.
- Bringing a creative and strategic mindset to the products I build.

> **My philosophy:** Learn → Build → Break → Debug → Improve → Repeat.
